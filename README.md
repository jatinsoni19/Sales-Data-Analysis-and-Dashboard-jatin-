# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes supermarket sales data using Power BI to provide insights into sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and product trends. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key business metrics and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track profit and revenue trends
- Analyze product category performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand customer purchasing patterns
- Support business growth through data-driven insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX

## 📈 Dashboard Features
The dashboard includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Product Category Performance
- Top Selling Products
- Regional Sales Analysis
- KPI Cards and Interactive Filters

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### Sales Performance Analysis
- Monitored monthly and yearly sales trends
- Compared revenue across product categories
- Identified peak sales periods

### Profitability Analysis
- Evaluated profit contribution by product category
- Identified high-profit and low-profit products
- Tracked overall business profitability

### Product Performance Analysis
- Identified top-selling products
- Compared sales performance across categories
- Analyzed customer purchasing trends

## 📊 Key Insights
- Identified best-performing product categories
- Highlighted products contributing most to revenue
- Revealed sales trends and seasonal patterns
- Supported business decision-making through visual analytics
